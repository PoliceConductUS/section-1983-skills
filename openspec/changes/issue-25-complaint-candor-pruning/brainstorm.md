# Brainstorm

## Problem

A complaint can obey the canonical skeleton and count mapping yet still harm
itself by volunteering an adverse merits assessment, expanding a fair-warning
unit into an authority brief, retaining uncertain facts that perform no pleaded
work, or acknowledging an ambiguous incorporated-record fact without resolving
its effect on an alternative offense actually in dispute.

The umbrella's no-concession rule does not make the independently usable general
complaint package self-contained on this point. Existing compression rules are
general and do not force the draft to explain why its own uncertainty labels
remain. Existing false-arrest rules require candor about incorporated material
and material alternative offenses but do not join those two duties at the count
level.

## Approved approach

- Put the filed-text no-concession rule in the canonical general complaint
  contract. Qualify evidence and factual uncertainty when accuracy requires it;
  do not volunteer adverse legal merits assessments. Preserve legitimate
  alternative and conditional pleading.
- Give the complaint-level fair-warning unit an operational default: one
  verified lead binding pre-event authority per distinct fair-warning
  proposition. An additional complaint-level authority must perform a separately
  identified job. Full comparisons and string cites remain internal.
- Add a completion-audit pruning ledger for paragraphs labeled unresolved,
  unknown, unrelated, or non-establishing. Each retained paragraph must serve an
  element, actual defense premise, chronology function, or preservation
  function.
- Join incorporated-record uncertainty to the false-arrest offense matrix. For
  an alternative offense actually raised by the defense, a ruling, or governing
  law, a material unresolved fact must be addressed without admitting it
  occurred or logged as a filing-critical GAP.

## Rejected alternatives

### Never hedge a claim

Taken literally, this could ban alternative pleading, conditional application,
or a candid source limitation. The contract instead prohibits volunteered
adverse merits assessments while preserving supported procedural alternatives.

### One case per count as an absolute maximum

A second authority can be necessary for a distinct proposition, offense
construction, precedential chain, or controlling limitation. The default is one
lead authority per fair-warning proposition, with every addition assigned a
separate job.

### Address every conceivable alternative offense

That would expand the pleading and invent defenses. The false-arrest delta
continues to limit the matrix to an offense actually raised by the defense, a
controlling ruling, or governing law.

### Put the checks in the linter

These are semantic complaint-construction duties, not mechanical prose signals.
Issue 26 separately owns linter exemptions, locations, and brief-creep warnings.

## Boundaries

- Use only generic synthetic examples and fixtures.
- Do not implement the CaseGraph checker or change the JSON mechanical handoff.
- Do not change judge-overlay execution, linter behavior, dependencies,
  workflows, root `docs/`, or `.superpowers/` content.

# Brainstorm

## Outcome

Preserve the repository's engineering and epistemic norms when another
contributor arrives. Publish one concise contribution contract and enforce only
its deterministic structure.

## Existing owners

- `GOVERNANCE.md` owns protected legal gates and human-controlled litigation
  judgment.
- `PUBLISHING.md` owns immutable tagged releases and the rule that `main` is not
  publication.
- The OpenSpec superpowers bridge owns the artifact lifecycle.

`CONTRIBUTING.md` links those owners instead of copying their registries or
release procedures.

## Missing contract

The contribution guide does not yet require one story per stacked branch,
RED/GREEN TDD, the full OpenSpec design/tasks/verification/retrospective cycle,
measurement as feedback rather than verdict, or the self-documenting-code and
comment rule.

## Enforcement boundary

Extend the existing standard-library governance validator to require stable
workflow phrases and confined owner links. Do not score writing quality, inspect
comment usefulness, create a governance body, or add a contributor license
agreement.

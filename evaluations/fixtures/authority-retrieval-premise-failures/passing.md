# Retrieval Frame

Frame `frame-001` limits the question to the Fictional Circuit rule effective
2025-06-15, Rule 12(b)(6), the supplied factual trigger and source universe, and
public-access limits through 2026-08-25. [cite:RETRIEVAL-BOUNDARY]

# Premise Review

The case identity is unresolved and remains a gap. The asserted holding is false
and is corrected before searching. The governing jurisdiction, provision, date,
and current validity each retain their own status and evidence.
[cite:RETRIEVAL-BOUNDARY]

# Candidate Handoff

Results preserve exact query, filters, system and product identity, execution
date, retrieval order, result identity, source type, artifact path, hash,
decision-date evidence, proposed legal role, and any stable rejection reason.
Every retained result remains a candidate for independent audit.
[cite:RETRIEVAL-BOUNDARY]

# Coverage Limits

The index omits sealed and unindexed matters. Empty or incomplete results cannot
support a claim of nonexistence or complete coverage. [cite:RETRIEVAL-BOUNDARY]

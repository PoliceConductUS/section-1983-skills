# Privilege-Log Requirements

The approved requirement calls for an entry identifier, request relationship,
date, author, recipients, document type, nonprivileged subject, asserted
protection, stated basis, custodian, and family relationship when applicable.
[cite:PL-REQUIREMENT]

# Entry Audit

## PL-1 — RFP 9

The supplied entry provides the date, recipient, document type, nonprivileged
subject, asserted protection, stated basis, and custodian. It does not provide
the author. No author is inferred, and the audit does not expose document
substance or adjudicate the asserted protection. [cite:PL-LOG]
[cite:PL-REQUIREMENT]

The missing author is a scoped log deficiency under the supplied requirement. It
does not itself establish waiver. [cite:PL-REQUIREMENT]

# Plaintiff Decisions

PLAINTIFF DECISION REQUIRED: Request a supplemented author field, confer about
the entry, preserve whether to advance a waiver argument for later
consideration, or take no present step. A cure may complete the log; conferral
may clarify the entry; preserving the question avoids deciding it now. No option
is selected.

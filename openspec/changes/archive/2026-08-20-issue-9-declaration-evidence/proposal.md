# Proposal: Section 1983 Declarations and Evidence

## Why

The repository routes summary-judgment work but does not provide a public
contract for converting a plaintiff's actual personal knowledge and
source-bounded exhibit relationship into a declaration. Without that contract,
record content, analysis, inference, legal conclusions, or expected discovery
can be misstated as firsthand fact, and an unsigned draft can bypass the human
approval required before execution.

## What changes

- Add one public declaration-and-evidence skill for factual human declarations
  and exhibit-foundation preparation.
- Classify every proposed proposition before declaration drafting and preserve
  the basis, source, exhibit, disposition, and approval state.
- Require statement-specific personal-knowledge and competency checks.
- Use the correct domestic or foreign 28 U.S.C. § 1746 form only after the
  actual execution location is supplied.
- Keep attributed records, derived analysis, inference, legal conclusions, and
  discovery expectations separate from firsthand testimony.
- Require explicit human declarant approval of every retained exact statement
  before execution readiness.
- Add focused structural tests, five generic synthetic fixture families with
  independently discriminating behavior regressions, and fresh public behavior
  evidence.

## Capabilities

### New capabilities

- `drafting-section-1983-declarations-and-evidence`: Prepare a source-bounded
  factual declaration, classification ledger, exhibit-foundation map, and human
  approval status without certifying admissibility or execution.

### Modified capabilities

None.

## Impact

The change adds one skill directory with OpenAI metadata, updates README and the
existing drafting and summary-judgment routes, and adds standard-library tests
and five synthetic fixture directories. It adds no dependency, script, private
case fact, signature, filing action, expert opinion, custodian certification,
admissibility ruling, or automatic litigation decision.

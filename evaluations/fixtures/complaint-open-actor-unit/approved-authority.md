# Approved Synthetic Authority

Criminal trespass is the only offense raised in the synthetic record. It
requires entry onto the identified property and notice that entry was forbidden.

Fictional binding pre-event Case A holds that seizing a person for criminal
trespass without facts supporting entry or notice violates the Fourth Amendment
and that the element-specific rule gives every reasonable officer fair warning.
Case A predates both challenged seizures. No other authority is approved for
this fixture.

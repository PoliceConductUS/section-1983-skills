## High-value independently reasoned decisions

- _Cornett v. Ward_, No. 3:18-cv-01395-S, ECF 46: pleaded force facts, qualified
  immunity, conspiracy.
- _Liggins v. Duncanville Texas_, No. 3:20-cv-00654-S, ECF 36 and 45:
  limitations, policymaker, custom, training, moving force, repeated amendment.
- _Madden v. Gribbon_, No. 3:21-cv-01168-S, ECF 47: underlying violation,
  pattern, single incident, ratification.
- _Alexander v. City of Dallas Texas_, No. 3:22-cv-01193-S, ECF 45: Monell
  comparators, retaliation, probable cause, training.
- _Niter v. Brown_, No. 3:23-cv-01621-S, ECF 33: policy versus violated
  regulation, policymaker, causation, qualified immunity.
- _Hodge v. Engleman_, No. 3:21-cv-01916-S, ECF 45: incorporated video, deadly
  force, fact-specific clearly established law.
- _Tafolla v. Rodermund_, No. 3:24-cv-00331-S, ECF 35: phase-specific force
  analysis and clearly established law.
- _Kipp v. Laubach_, No. 3:17-cv-00067-S, ECF 99: factual disputes at summary
  judgment and qualified immunity.

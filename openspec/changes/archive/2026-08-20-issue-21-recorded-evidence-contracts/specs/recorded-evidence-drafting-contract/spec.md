## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recorded-event assertions remain source bounded

Complaint and Rule 59(e) drafting contracts SHALL present an event or conduct
during a recorded interval as established by the recording only when the event
or conduct is visible in that recording.

#### Scenario: Recording does not resolve the alleged event

- **WHEN** the available recording does not show or resolve the alleged event
- **THEN** the filed text may retain the allegation only by identifying present
  recollection, stating that available recordings do not resolve it, and making
  the allegation subject to correction if additional recordings are produced or
  located
- **AND** the contract reports a validation failure or gap when neither route is
  complete

### Requirement: Recorded statements remain transcript bounded

Complaint and Rule 59(e) drafting contracts SHALL present a quoted, paraphrased,
or attributed recorded statement as established by the recording only when it
appears in a verified transcript. Exact quotations SHALL match, paraphrases
SHALL add no content, and uncertain speaker attribution SHALL remain uncertain.

#### Scenario: Transcript does not resolve the alleged statement

- **WHEN** the verified transcript does not establish the alleged statement
- **THEN** the same present-recollection, unresolved-recording, and later-
  correction route applies
- **AND** the contract reports a validation failure or gap when neither route is
  complete

### Requirement: Rule 59 final-review numbering remains continuous

The Rule 59(e) final-review acceptance list SHALL include the recorded-evidence
gate and SHALL retain a continuous ordered sequence.

#### Scenario: Recorded-evidence checkpoint is inserted

- **WHEN** the recorded-evidence checkpoint appears in the final-review list
- **THEN** every list item has the next consecutive number with no duplicate or
  skipped value

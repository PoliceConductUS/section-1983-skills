# Implementation Plan

## Objective

Deliver a public, test-backed defense-counsel overlay skill that converts one
immutable approved public-source snapshot into separately attributable identity,
team behavior, judicial-treatment, current-attack-link, and calibrated forecast
records without personal profiling, unsupported authorship, or certainty.

## Task 1: Public structure RED

Add one focused structural test for the exact skill package, two install-local
schemas, standard-library validator, generic fixtures, README and drafting-
router routes, governance registration, `COUNSEL_OVERLAYS.md`, the general-guide
link, and the two new filing-manifest kinds. Prove RED is caused only by absent
Issue 30 artifacts.

## Task 2: Attribution and source RED

Create generic synthetic in-test builders and committed fixtures for a complete
public-source snapshot and counsel overlay. Exercise the validator through its
public CLI. Lock down stable IDs, canonical fingerprints, source roles, checked
dates, research queries, coverage and fee-gated gaps, attorney identity, team
membership, exact professional attribution, and source-supported behavior.

Add mutations proving a listed or appearance attorney cannot inherit joint-
filing authorship, firm biographies and CLE material cannot establish behavior,
and judicial treatment cannot become counsel conduct.

## Task 3: Pattern, forecast, and review RED

Add complete and incomplete comparable corpora. Prove recurring patterns, loss
patterns, and forecasts require denominators, missingness, posture, support,
contrary evidence, confidence, sources, and checked-through date. Reject
certainty, personal profiling, case-outcome prediction, automatic estoppel, and
provenance-rewriting overrides.

Extend review-plan tests so blind jobs reject all counsel material, actual jobs
accept only the target's effective relevant team slice, and forecasts cannot
remove common attacks.

## Task 4: Minimal public skill and validator

Add `building-defense-counsel-overlays` with `SKILL.md`, metadata, the two
schemas, validator, and generic fixtures. Keep implementation standard-library-
only, fail closed on malformed nested types, emit stable findings, and add no
unrelated helper or dependency.

## Task 5: Documentation and composition

Add `COUNSEL_OVERLAYS.md` with source, attribution, lifecycle, calibration,
substitution, and team-realignment rules. Update README, the drafting router,
`OVERLAYS.md`, governance registry, filing-manifest schema, and litigation-
alignment validator with the exact approved composition boundaries.

## Task 6: GREEN, pressure, and review

Run focused tests until GREEN. Exercise the complete and incomplete synthetic
corpora through the public CLI. Run quick skill validation, discovery, all
unit/evaluation tests, OpenSpec, corpus, governance, formatting, forbidden-
folder, comment, private-marker, and diff checks. Inspect attribution, corpus
strength, forecast, review isolation, lifecycle, and public-safety seams.
Capture every accepted Critical or Important finding as RED before correction.
Commit and sync each test, implementation, and correction boundary.

## Task 7: Verify and archive

Complete verification and retrospective records, archive with the repository-
local OpenSpec CLI, replace any generated durable Purpose placeholder, validate
both durable capabilities, commit, sync, and finish with a clean local/origin-
equal branch, Issue 30 open, and no PR.

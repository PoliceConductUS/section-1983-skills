# Retrospective

## Outcome

Issue 30 turns a bounded public-source research packet into an immutable
professional litigation profile for defense attorneys and time-bounded counsel
teams. It permits a calibrated next-move forecast without converting appearance
into authorship, court action into counsel conduct, public-record gaps into a
complete denominator, or prediction into fact.

## What RED established

The repository could map the current docket's attacks to litigation-alignment
groups, but it had no public contract for reusable attorney identity, team-level
attribution, cross-case professional behavior, court-documented treatment, or
forecast strength. Filing manifests also could not distinguish individual-
identity and team-behavior pins.

## What worked

- Separating identity from behavior kept bar and firm sources from becoming
  litigation evidence.
- Team-level default attribution captured what a joint paper actually proves
  without assigning authorship to every listed lawyer.
- Separate historical, judicial-treatment, current-attack, and forecast layers
  made each actor and inference independently reviewable.
- A complete comparable-set contract made missing public dockets visible and
  forced forecasts to retain contrary evidence and calibrated limits.
- Linking counsel review slices to existing job IDs preserved the blind common-
  attack pass without duplicating the litigation-alignment review plan.

## Review findings and corrections

The first GREEN pass exposed a nested unhashable `team_id` and a generic finding
for suppressed common attacks. Schema pressure then showed that the overlay
schema had not yet published its nested record shapes. Whole-story review found
unreconciled nested identity sources, team source dates, pattern and forecast
source unions, and current-attack sources. A systematic malformed-leaf sweep
found 28 list/object mutations that could escape as Python exceptions. The final
integration review found that counsel pin validation was available only through
the module API, not the public CLI. Each accepted finding became RED before its
correction.

## Deviations

- The skill does not perform live research. A separately authorized research
  preflight builds the immutable embedded snapshot; validation is offline and
  fee-free.
- The validator does not decide whether an argument is legally correct, whether
  a forecast is persuasive, or whether cross-case differences create a legal
  effect. Those remain separate legal-analysis questions.
- Historical public records may remain incomplete even after a diligent search.
  The contract prefers bounded examples and explicit gaps over a denominator-
  free tendency.

## Reusable lesson

Professional litigation intelligence needs two kinds of restraint at once:
strict attribution of what happened and calibrated uncertainty about what may
happen next. Provenance alone does not justify a forecast, and a pattern alone
does not justify certainty. Both must travel with their denominator,
missingness, contrary evidence, posture, and limits.

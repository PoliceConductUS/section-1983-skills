# Approved Synthetic Log Requirement

The supplied requirement calls for entry identifier, request relationship, date,
author, recipients, document type, nonprivileged subject, asserted privilege or
protection, stated basis, custodian, and family relationship when applicable.
Identify a missing field without inferring it. A missing field does not itself
establish waiver. Whether to assert a waiver theory, seek a cure, confer, or
move remains the plaintiff's choice.

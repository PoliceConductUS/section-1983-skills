# Count I — False Arrest

## Officer Alder

For Alder's challenged 10:00 seizure, Plaintiff incorporates paragraphs 20–24.
Alder then knew only that an anonymous caller had reported a person in a red
coat near a closed store. Alder saw Plaintiff wearing blue at a public bus stop,
saw no entry, and knew of no notice forbidding entry. Criminal trespass required
both entry and notice. Alder's then-known facts supplied neither element, so he
lacked probable cause and arguable probable cause. Under QI prong one, the
unsupported seizure violated the Fourth Amendment. Under QI prong two, pre-event
Case A matched the absent entry-and-notice facts and gave Alder conduct-specific
fair warning. Alder personally caused Plaintiff's loss of liberty beginning at
10:00. [cite:ACTOR-FACTS] [cite:ACTOR-AUTHORITY]

## Officer Birch

For Birch's challenged continued seizure from 10:04 through 10:25, Plaintiff
incorporates paragraphs 25–30. Birch then knew that the caller had retracted the
report, saw no entry, and knew of no notice forbidding entry. Those facts
supplied neither required trespass element, so continuing the handcuffed seizure
lacked probable cause and arguable probable cause. Under QI prong one, Birch's
continued seizure violated the Fourth Amendment. Under QI prong two, pre-event
Case A matched the absent entry-and-notice facts and gave Birch conduct-specific
fair warning. Birch personally caused Plaintiff's handcuffed loss of liberty
from 10:04 through 10:25. [cite:ACTOR-FACTS] [cite:ACTOR-AUTHORITY]

## Later-Fact Boundary

Paragraphs 31–34 perform only later chronology and report functions. Alder's
11:30 report and the later charging decision were not part of either officer's
knowledge at the challenged seizure point and do not justify either earlier
seizure. [cite:ACTOR-FACTS]

## Causation and Injury

Alder caused the initial seizure beginning at 10:00. Birch separately caused the
continued handcuffed seizure from 10:04 through 10:25. Each act caused the loss
of liberty attributed above; no other injury is alleged in this synthetic count.

# Banned words and phrases — consolidated

Union of the word-avoidance lists from all sources. Every entry is banned in
complaint prose. Source tags: [STE] = the ste-writing skill and its linter;
[Horan] = _Bad Words_ (Horan); [McAlpin] = _Beyond the First Draft_ (McAlpin).
This file and `scripts/banned_terms.py` are generated from the same inventory;
edit the generator inputs, not the lists.

## Exemptions (never flag these)

Terms of art keep their required wording even when they contain a banned word.
Quoted record material and quoted statutory or case text are also exempt.

jurisdiction, genuine dispute, genuine issue, gross negligence, grossly

## Empty abstract nouns and sentence openers [McAlpin]

Do not open a sentence with: it is, there are, there is, the nature of, the fact
that, the situation, the issue of, the question of

## Vague verbs [McAlpin]

involve, concern, deal with, relate to, address (when a concrete verb exists)

## Rhetorical devices and punctuation [Horan] [STE]

- No exclamation marks. [Horan]
- No rhetorical questions; state the point as a declarative sentence. [Horan]
- No semicolons, em dashes, or contractions. [STE]

## Mechanics [STE]

Sentences: 25-word cap. Paragraphs: one set of circumstances each.

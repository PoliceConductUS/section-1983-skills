# Response in support of a report and recommendation

When the magistrate judge recommends in the plaintiff's favor and the
defendant objects, the response defends the R&R. This is the mirror of
`rr-objection.md`.

## Deadline

14 days from service of the objections. Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b)(2). Add 3
days for mail service. Rule 6(d).

## Standard of review (the law's voice)

De novo review, but only of the portions properly objected to; general
objections preserve nothing, and arguments not presented to the
magistrate judge are ordinarily waived. Recite with the forum circuit's
authority from the authorities protocol.

## Skeleton

1. Caption and title identifying the R&R and the objections by docket
   number
2. Introduction: what the R&R correctly concluded and what the court
   should do (overrule the objections and adopt the R&R)
3. Standard of review
4. Response, one section per objection, in the objections' order:
   a. State the objection
   b. Show it is waived, too general to trigger review, or wrong on the
   record and the law, citing the R&R's pages and reasoning
5. Alternative grounds: if the record supports the R&R's result for
   reasons the R&R did not reach, offer them; a correct result can be
   affirmed on any ground the record supports
6. Conclusion: adopt the R&R in full

## Discipline rules

- Defend the reasoning, not just the result; the district judge is
  deciding whether to sign onto the R&R's analysis.
- Point out waiver first where it applies. An objection raising a new
  argument is answered by the waiver rule before the merits.
- Do not re-argue points the defendant did not object to; unobjected
  portions stand on clear-error review and need no help.

# Document Attack Checklists

## Universal Attack Checklist

Apply every universal attack before the selected document-family checklist:

- Confirm the caption, party names and capacities, document title, docket
  references, signature, requested relief, and internal cross-references agree.
- Identify the supplied procedural posture, governing deadline, approved federal
  and local rules, standing orders, page limits, certifications, and required
  filing components. Flag a required item absent from the approved packet as a
  scoped gap.
- Test every load-bearing factual proposition against an approved source. Keep
  personal knowledge, attributed record facts, supported inferences, legal
  applications, and adverse characterizations distinct.
- Test every load-bearing legal proposition against the approved authorities and
  their stated roles. Flag apparent identity, status, quotation, pinpoint, or
  posture problems for `audit-authorities`; do not certify them here.
- Check chronology, actor attribution, relevant-time knowledge, causation,
  injury, source conflicts, and later facts used to justify an earlier decision.
- Identify each unapproved concession, abandoned response, unsupported adverse
  characterization, and mismatch between the argument and requested ruling.
- Review every incorporated or attached item for defense-favorable language,
  ambiguity, completeness, and the risk that it controls over the draft.
- Separate a filing-critical procedural omission, a plausible nonfatal opponent
  attack, a factual conflict, opponent-controlled discovery, and a style point
  into the report's five distinct categories.

## Complaint or amended complaint

- Check the Rule 8 and Rule 10 spine: jurisdiction, venue, parties, capacity,
  color of law, chronological facts, separate counts, injury, relief, jury
  demand, and signature.
- For every count and defendant, map the constitutional right, required
  elements, decisive facts, relevant-time knowledge, application, participation,
  causation, injury, and result. Attack group pleading and omnibus
  incorporation.
- Check that personal knowledge, attributed records, information-and-belief
  allegations, and non-obvious inferences have the support and attribution the
  draft claims.
- For each qualified-immunity-eligible defendant, test both prongs and the
  concise complaint-level fair-warning unit against approved binding pre-event
  authority, material similarities, differences, and conduct-level specificity.
- Test each Monell path separately for an identified policy or custom, concrete
  facts, municipal inference, attribution and notice, particular injury, and
  moving-force mechanism. Attack theory lists that defer the entire basis to
  discovery.
- Check false arrest and related encounter stages for the seizure boundary,
  offense elements, alternative offenses actually raised, actor-specific
  arrest-time knowledge, and later-event contamination.
- Check challenged statements and omissions for statement, contradiction,
  corrected account, materiality, author, transmission, timing, and causal role.
- For an amended complaint, check supersession, preservation of every supported
  allegation and count, exact cures, stable cross-references, and required
  redline or complete proposed pleading.

## Motion-to-dismiss response

- Inventory every actual dismissal ground and attacked claim or defendant. Check
  that the response answers each ground in the motion's order, while resolving a
  Rule 12(b)(1) jurisdictional attack before Rule 12(b)(6) merits arguments.
- Check the standard of review, burden, complaint version, incorporated
  material, judicial-notice boundary, and any conversion risk under Rule 12(d).
- Test every factual statement against a cited complaint paragraph or other
  posture-permitted approved source. New merits facts belong in an amended
  complaint, not the response.
- For each claim and defendant, test element-level application, personal
  participation, causation, injury, and both qualified-immunity prongs where
  raised.
- Compare the response's description of each defense premise and authority with
  the approved motion and source; attack straw arguments and unanswered fallback
  theories.
- Check the exact requested ruling by count and defendant. If amendment is
  sought alternatively, test whether the filing identifies the defect, exact
  cure, location, nonfutility basis, and any separate-motion requirement.

## Summary-judgment response

- Check the controlling local-rule format for the response to the movant's
  facts, additional facts, appendix, objections, and memorandum. Flag any
  omission that could deem a fact admitted or evidence unconsidered.
- Test every admit, deny, or qualification against a precise approved record
  cite. An unsupported dispute is an admission risk; a pleading is not
  summary-judgment evidence.
- Check that declarations, depositions, recordings, exhibits, and other evidence
  are competent for the stated proposition and that authentication,
  completeness, hearsay, personal knowledge, and best-evidence attacks are
  answered when the approved sources make them material.
- Map each attacked element to the evidence creating a genuine material dispute,
  preserving the nonmovant inference standard without treating argument as fact.
- Test both qualified-immunity prongs with claim-, defendant-, conduct-, and
  event-specific record and authority support.
- If material discovery is unavailable, check a Rule 56(d) declaration for the
  specific facts sought, why they are unavailable, what discovery will obtain
  them, and how they will create a material dispute.
- Check the requested disposition of each claim and defendant and consistency
  among the fact response, additional facts, memorandum, and proposed order.

## Leave to amend

- Determine from approved dates and filings whether amendment is available as of
  right, requires Rule 15 leave, or also requires Rule 16 good cause after a
  scheduling-order deadline.
- Check timeliness, diligence, amendment history, explanation for delay, bad
  faith, prejudice, repeated failure to cure, futility, and any governing local
  conference requirement.
- Identify every proposed change by claim, defendant, fact, and relief; test the
  motion's summary against the complete proposed pleading and any required
  redline.
- For each asserted cure, check the exact prior defect, copy-ready cure,
  location in the proposed pleading, supporting approved source, and
  claim-specific nonfutility analysis.
- Check that the proposed amended complaint is complete and superseding rather
  than a supplement that silently drops supported allegations, claims, parties,
  damages, or jury demand.
- Check the requested order, attachments, certificate, deadline, and whether the
  motion asks only for relief the supplied rules permit.

## Extension motion

- Identify the deadline, its source, the filing date, and requested new date.
  Determine whether Rule 6 good cause or post-deadline excusable neglect
  governs.
- Test the stated reason against concrete approved facts, the time actually
  needed, diligence, foreseeability, control, and whether the request would
  force another extension.
- Check every prior extension of the same deadline and consistency with the
  docket and scheduling order.
- Check the opposing party's position and every approved meet-and-confer or
  certificate requirement; do not infer consent from silence.
- Test claimed lack of prejudice against trial, hearing, response, and other
  schedule dates in the approved record.
- Check the exact relief, proposed order, certificate of service, and any local
  filing component.

## R&R objection

- Verify the objection deadline, service method, filing date, and any extension
  against the approved record.
- Select the Rule 72(a) or Rule 72(b) standard from the supplied disposition and
  attack use of the wrong review standard.
- Check that each numbered objection identifies an exact R&R finding or
  conclusion and page, explains the error from the record and approved
  authority, and states the result the district judge should reach.
- Attack general objections, mere repetition, harmless points, and objections
  that fail to preserve the dispositive issue.
- Identify arguments or evidence not presented to the magistrate judge and apply
  the approved waiver or new-matter rules without deciding disputed facts.
- Check adverse descriptions for attribution and unapproved concessions.
- Check that the conclusion requests rejection or modification of specified R&R
  portions and a concrete claim- and defendant-specific ruling.

## R&R response

- Verify the response deadline, service method, governing standard, and
  permitted scope against the approved record.
- Inventory each actual objection in order and check that the response addresses
  its specific premise without rearguing unobjected portions.
- Test whether an objection is specific enough to trigger review, preserves the
  issue, or raises a new argument; apply waiver before merits only when approved
  authority supports it.
- Defend the R&R's reasoning, record support, and legal path rather than
  asserting only that its result is correct.
- Check every alternative ground against the record before the magistrate judge
  and the supplied authority; attack a new factual or legal theory outside that
  boundary.
- Check adverse descriptions for attribution, factual disputes for accurate
  treatment, and every source citation for the role claimed.
- Check that the conclusion asks for the exact supported disposition, including
  which objections to overrule and which R&R portions to adopt.
